How Much Does Home Care Cost in Houston? (2026 Guide)

What families pay for in-home care in greater Houston, what drives the price, and how to pay for it.

If you're researching care for a parent or spouse, cost is probably your first question — and it's a fair one. The honest answer is that in-home care is priced by the hour, and the total depends on three things: how many hours you need, what level of care those hours require, and when the care happens.

The three cost drivers

Hours per week. A few mornings a week for bathing and breakfast is the most affordable way to start. Daily support and around-the-clock coverage scale up from there.

Level of care. Non-medical personal assistance — bathing, meals, housekeeping, companionship — is priced differently from skilled nursing such as wound care or medication management, which requires a licensed nurse and a physician's plan of care.

Schedule. Overnights, weekends, and holidays may carry different rates because of staffing.

Why agencies rarely post prices

A 'starting at' rate is rarely what a family actually pays, because every situation is different. A better approach — and the one we use — is a free in-home assessment followed by a written, itemized quote you can adjust as needs change.

How Houston families pay

  • Private pay — most common and most flexible.
  • Long-term care insurance — many policies cover in-home care once a waiting period is met.
  • Medicaid programs — Texas Medicaid and waiver programs may cover personal assistance for eligible people.
  • Medicare and health plans — may cover skilled home health for eligible patients through certified agencies.
  • VA benefits — Aid & Attendance can help eligible veterans and surviving spouses.

Questions to ask any agency

  • Is the rate hourly, and is there a minimum?
  • Is the agency licensed by Texas HHSC? (Ask for the license number.)
  • Are caregivers employees who are background-checked and supervised?
  • Is there a long-term contract?
  • What happens if a caregiver is sick?
