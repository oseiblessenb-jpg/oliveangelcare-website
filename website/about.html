Our story

Care born from family, built for yours

Olive Angel Care Services was founded by a family of healthcare professionals who believe everyone deserves to age, heal, and live with dignity at home — surrounded by warmth and treated with respect.

Our mission

To provide compassionate, high-quality home and community care that helps the people of greater Houston live safely and comfortably in the place they love most — home. We pair clinical excellence with genuine human kindness, treating every client like one of our own.

What we believe

  • Dignity — every person deserves respect, choice, and comfort.
  • Compassion — we lead with empathy in every interaction.
  • Excellence — nurse-led, accountable, high-standard care.
  • Integrity — honest, transparent, and dependable.
  • Family — we partner with families, not just clients.
