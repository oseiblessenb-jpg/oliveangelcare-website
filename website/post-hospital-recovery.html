Care After a Hospital Stay

Recovering at home after surgery or a hospital stay.

Request a free assessmentCall 832-759-0766

How we help

Coming home is a relief — and a risky moment. Missed medications, a fall, or a wound that isn't healing are the most common reasons people end up back in the hospital. We help families get through the first weeks safely.

What's included

  • Skilled nursing visits under your physician's plan of care
  • Medication reconciliation, setup, and education
  • Wound care and dressing changes
  • Mobility and fall-risk support around the home
  • Help with bathing, meals, and errands while you recover
  • Coordination with your doctors and discharge planners

Is this the right fit?

  • You or a loved one is being discharged after surgery or a hospitalization
  • A doctor recommended nursing or therapy at home
  • You're worried about managing new medications or a wound
  • The family can't be there around the clock during recovery
Your privacy matters. Please don't share medical details through this website — we gather anything clinical securely by phone. In an emergency, call 911.
Common questions

Frequently asked

How quickly can care start after discharge?

Often within a day or two once we have your physician's order — call us before discharge if you can and we'll coordinate with the hospital.

Do I need a doctor's order?

For skilled home health, yes. Personal assistance (help with daily living) does not require one.

Can you help prevent a readmission?

That's the goal: medication review, early warning-sign monitoring, safe mobility, and clear communication with your care team.

Let's talk about what would help most

A warm, no-pressure conversation — and a free in-home assessment when you're ready.
