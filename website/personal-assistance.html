Personal Assistance Services

A caring, dependable helping hand with the activities of daily life — so your loved one can stay safe, comfortable, and independent at home.

Request CareCall 832-759-0766

Help with the everyday — and peace of mind for the family

Personal Assistance Services (PAS) support people who need a hand with day-to-day living but not skilled medical care. Our attendants bring patience, reliability, and warmth into the home, helping with the tasks that keep life comfortable and dignified.

Whether you need a few hours a week or daily support, we tailor a schedule that fits your family — and give caregivers a chance to rest knowing their loved one is in good hands.

What our attendants help with

Personal care

Bathing, grooming, dressing, toileting, and mobility assistance — always with dignity and respect.

Home & meals

Meal preparation, light housekeeping, laundry, and keeping living spaces tidy and safe.

Errands & outings

Grocery shopping, prescription pickups, appointments, and accompaniment around town.

Companionship

Conversation, hobbies, walks, and a friendly presence that brightens the day.

Reminders

Gentle prompts for medications, meals, hydration, and daily routines.

Respite for families

Short-term coverage so family caregivers can rest, work, or simply recharge.

Is it right for you?

Who personal assistance is for

Personal Assistance Services are a good fit when a loved one needs a dependable helping hand with daily life — but not skilled medical care.

  • Needs help with bathing, dressing, grooming, or mobility
  • Could use support with meals, light housekeeping, and laundry
  • Needs rides and accompaniment to errands or appointments
  • Would benefit from companionship and a friendly routine
  • Needs gentle reminders for meals, hydration, and daily tasks
  • A family caregiver who needs respite and peace of mind

Common questions

Frequently asked

Is personal assistance medical care?

No. Personal Assistance Services are non-medical. Our attendants help with everyday living — personal care, meals, errands, and companionship. If skilled clinical care is needed, we can talk through home health options too.

Do I need a doctor's order?

No. Unlike skilled home health, personal assistance does not require a physician's order. Just reach out and we'll build a plan around your needs.

How many hours can we get?

Schedules are flexible — from a few hours a week to daily support. We'll tailor a plan that fits your family's needs and budget.

Can attendants drive my loved one to appointments?

Yes. Accompaniment to errands, appointments, and outings is part of what our attendants help with.

How are attendants screened?

Every caregiver is background-checked and trained, and our care is overseen by experienced leadership for quality and consistency.
