Common questions

Everything you're wondering

How do we get started with care?

Reach out by phone or request a callback. We'll listen to your situation, answer questions, and discuss any health needs privately by phone. Then we build a personalized plan and match you with the right caregivers.

Do you provide medical and non-medical care?

Yes. We offer skilled home health (nursing and clinical care), non-medical personal assistance (help with daily living) — so we can adapt as needs change.

What areas do you serve?

We serve families across greater Houston — Harris, Fort Bend, and Brazoria counties. Not sure if you're in our area? Just give us a call.

Are your caregivers screened and trained?

Absolutely. Every caregiver is background-checked and trained, and clinical care is supervised by a registered nurse.

Are you licensed?

Yes. Olive Angel Care Services is a licensed Texas Home & Community Support Services Agency, now accepting clients across greater Houston.

How do I share health information safely?

Please don't enter medical details into our website forms. Our online forms collect only basic contact info; we gather anything clinical securely by phone. In an emergency, call 911.

Ready to talk about care?

Reach our team directly — we'll listen and help you find the right next step.
