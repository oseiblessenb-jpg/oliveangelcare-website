For new team members

Welcome to Olive Angel

We're so glad you're here. This page walks you through everything you need for a smooth first week. Work through each section at your own pace — and reach out anytime you have questions.

Heads up: This is a private resource area for staff. Document links below will connect to our secure HR system once onboarding tools are live. For now, your administrator will share files directly.
Step 1

Your paperwork checklist

Have these ready or completed before your start date.

Identity & eligibility

  • Signed offer letter
  • Form I-9 + ID documents
  • W-4 tax withholding
  • Direct deposit form

Credentials & screening

  • Copy of active license/certification (RN, LVN, HHA)
  • Background check authorization
  • TB test / health screening records
  • CPR certification (if applicable)

Policies to review & sign

  • Employee handbook acknowledgment
  • HIPAA & confidentiality agreement
  • Code of conduct
  • Client rights & responsibilities

Getting set up

  • Emergency contact form
  • Scheduling app access
  • Direct supervisor introduction
  • Office/parking and key info
Step 2

Training

Complete the assigned modules before your first client visit. Your administrator will confirm which apply to your role.

Orientation

Mission, values, and how we deliver care at Olive Angel.

Compliance & safety

HIPAA, infection control, bloodborne pathogens, and incident reporting.

Client rights & dignity

Respect, abuse/neglect prevention, and person-centered care.

Documentation

How to record visits accurately and on time.

Emergency procedures

What to do — and who to call — in urgent situations.

Role-specific skills

Clinical or attendant competencies for your position.

Step 3

Your first week

Day 1 — Orientation

Meet your team, finish paperwork, and get your schedule and tools set up.

Days 2–3 — Training

Complete your assigned modules and review policies and procedures.

Days 4–5 — Shadowing

Observe and assist alongside an experienced team member.

Week's end — Check-in

Sit down with your supervisor to ask questions and confirm you're ready.

Need help?

Your administrator and supervising nurse are your go-to contacts during onboarding.
