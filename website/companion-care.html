Companion Care

Friendly, dependable company — and a helping hand.

Request a free assessmentCall 832-759-0766

How we help

Loneliness affects health as much as any diagnosis. Our companion caregivers bring conversation, routine, and light help around the house so your loved one stays engaged, safe, and connected.

What's included

  • Conversation, hobbies, games, and walks
  • Meal preparation and shared mealtimes
  • Light housekeeping and laundry
  • Errands, grocery shopping, and appointment accompaniment
  • Medication and hydration reminders
  • Regular check-ins for the family

Is this the right fit?

  • A parent lives alone and seems isolated or low
  • Daily tasks are slipping — meals, mail, tidying
  • You live far away and want someone reliable checking in
Your privacy matters. Please don't share medical details through this website — we gather anything clinical securely by phone. In an emergency, call 911.
Common questions

Frequently asked

Is companion care medical?

No. It's non-medical support with daily life and social connection. If skilled care is ever needed, we can add it.

Can companions drive my loved one?

Yes — accompaniment to errands and appointments is part of what we do.

How many hours can we schedule?

From a couple of hours a week to daily visits — we'll fit your family's needs and budget.

Let's talk about what would help most

A warm, no-pressure conversation — and a free in-home assessment when you're ready.
