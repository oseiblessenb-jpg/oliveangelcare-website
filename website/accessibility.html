Accessibility

Everyone deserves to find care with ease. We're committed to making this website usable for all visitors.

Our commitment

Olive Angel Care Services is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, and we continually work to improve the experience for everyone.

What we do

  • Clear, readable text with strong color contrast
  • Full keyboard navigation and visible focus indicators
  • Descriptive alternative text for meaningful images
  • Labels and structure that work with screen readers
  • Responsive layouts that adapt to phones, tablets, and desktops
  • A "skip to content" link on every page

Need this information another way?

If you have trouble accessing any part of this site, or need information in a different format, we're glad to help. Call 832-759-0766 or email info@oliveangelcareservices.com and a member of our team will assist you directly.

Ongoing work

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We welcome your feedback — if something isn't working well for you, please let us know so we can fix it.
